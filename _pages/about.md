I’m Boyan Tong, a student at Wuhan Britain-China School. I’m interested in sociology and in how work, technology and institutions shape people’s health and everyday lives.

My interest began with pneumoconiosis. Meeting former miners and their families in Pingxiang showed me how an occupational disease reaches far beyond the lungs—into family roles, household finances, children’s schooling and people’s ability to claim support. Since then I have approached the same question from several directions: policy research and data analysis, interviews and reporting, documentary film, and designing devices and installations that make hidden risks visible.

## Learning

- **Pioneer Academics** — STS research on chronic-disease self-management apps, completed as a full research paper.
- **Harvard Secondary School Program** — *Self, Society & Politics*, where I wrote a final paper on the inclusiveness of civic nationalism; and *Disease, Illness & Health Through Literature*, where I studied illness narratives, chronic-disease stigma and patient autonomy and wrote a 2,400-word creative dialogue.

## This blog

Here I share research notes and data, oral histories from fieldwork, reports on pneumoconiosis and commentary on wider social issues.
