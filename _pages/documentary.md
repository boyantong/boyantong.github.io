## Borrowed Health: A Deferred Cost

<p class="project__meta">Director, producer and editor · Five-student team · 12 minutes</p>
<div class="tag-row"><span>Documentary</span><span>Interviews</span><span>Occupational disease</span></div>

{% include video id="m986Ctg_jD0" provider="youtube" %}

*Borrowed Health: A Deferred Cost* opens at the Wuhan Yangtze River Bridge and explores the costs of industrial labor that persist after the work ends. Illness limits movement, changes close relationships and leaves individuals to shoulder medical expenses. Following three workers’ employment histories and efforts to seek help, the film asks who should take responsibility for the lasting harm to their health and the care they need, even as the material achievements of development endure.

I initiated and directed this documentary with a team of five students. I developed the interview framework and filming script, coordinated with occupational disease departments and physicians in Wuhan, and interviewed the three participants. I also produced and edited the film, taking the project from initial planning through the final cut.
