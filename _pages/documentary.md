## Borrowed Health: A Deferred Cost

<p class="project__meta">Founder & director · 5-student team · 12 minutes</p>
<div class="tag-row"><span>Documentary</span><span>Interviews</span><span>Occupational disease</span></div>

{% include video id="m986Ctg_jD0" provider="youtube" %}

*Borrowed Health* follows three people living with pneumoconiosis: Zhang Hua, Tao Lujun and Xu Guohe. Through their accounts, the film shows how industrial work continues to shape daily movement, family relationships and financial obligations long after exposure has ended.

I founded and directed the project with a team of five students. I developed the interview framework and filming script, coordinated with occupational-disease departments and physicians in Wuhan, interviewed the three patients, and produced and edited the film. It connects the material achievements of labour with the health costs that workers and their families continue to bear.
